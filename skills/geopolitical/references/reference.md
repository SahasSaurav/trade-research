# Geopolitical reference

## Company connection types (detailed)

**`revenue_exposure`:** Event affects a region where the company has significant revenue exposure

- Example: Economic slowdown in a region representing 15%+ of revenue

**`operations`:** Event directly affects the company's manufacturing, distribution, or offices

- Example: Conflict in a country where the company has factories

**`suppliers`:** Event affects the company's supply chain or critical suppliers

- Example: Export controls on components from the company's key suppliers

**`customers`:** Event affects the company's major customers or their purchasing power

- Example: Economic crisis reducing enterprise customer spending

**`logistics`:** Event affects shipping routes or transportation infrastructure the company relies on

- Example: Port closure affecting the company's distribution

**`commodities`:** Event affects the price or supply of critical raw materials or commodities

- Example: Rare earth supply disruption affecting semiconductor or tech manufacturing

**`regulation`:** Event introduces regulatory requirements the company must comply with

- Example: New data privacy law requiring changes to product features

**`market_access`:** Event restricts the company's ability to access or sell into a market

- Example: Trade embargo on a region where the company has sales

**`none`:** Event has no credible company connection

- **Action:** Exclude the entry entirely
