# News sentiment reference

## Material company-specific evidence

**Include:**

- Earnings results (beat, miss, in-line) and guidance (raise, cut, maintained)
- Analyst estimate or rating changes
- Product launches, delays, discontinuations
- Major wins (large customer, contract) or losses (major customer departure)
- Acquisitions, divestitures, partnerships
- Regulatory approvals (FDA, antitrust) or investigations
- Supply-chain disruptions or improvements
- CEO changes or C-suite departures
- Insider buying/selling (if material)

**Exclude:**

- Generic market commentary ("tech stocks rally")
- Company mentioned only in passing in a market report
- Speculation ("sources say", "may announce", "expected to")
- Historical anniversaries or routine events
- Analyst price target changes without a rating change

## Source priority hierarchy

### Tier 1 — Direct sources (preferred)

1. **Company press releases** — official announcements
2. **SEC filings** — 10-K, 10-Q, 8-K, earnings releases
3. **Company earnings call transcripts** — official management commentary

### Tier 2 — Major wire services and business press

1. **Reuters, Associated Press (AP)** — wire service standard
2. **Bloomberg, Financial Times** — major business outlets with independent reporters
3. **CNBC, Wall Street Journal, MarketWatch** — major business media

### Tier 3 — Trade and specialist press

1. **Trade journals** (e.g., semiconductor trade press for chip companies)
2. **Specialized outlets** (e.g., MacRumors for Apple)

### Tier 4 — Aggregators (use cautiously)

1. **Seeking Alpha** — include only if named analyst with credentials
2. **StockTwits, Reddit** — exclude (not credible)

**Rule:** Prefer Tier 1–2. Use Tier 3 for industry-specific insights. Avoid Tier 4 unless amplifying Tier 1–2.

## Recency standard

| Recency         | Treatment                                                          |
| --------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Last 7 days** | Fresh; include all credible coverage                               |
| **8–30 days**   | Good; include, esp. if material                                    |
| **31–90 days**  | Acceptable, but flag if older alternatives exist                   |
| **>90 days**    | Stale; exclude unless no fresher coverage and event still relevant |

**Seasonality note:** Flag seasonal factors in the summary (e.g., retail strong in Q4).
